## 2026-07-05 - Profile Visibility and Emoji Accessibility
**Learning:** In GitHub profile repositories, the entry point must be `README.md` for it to render on the profile page. Additionally, wrapping emojis in `<span role="img" aria-label="...">` is the standard for ensuring they are accessible to screen readers in Markdown.
**Action:** Always verify that profile entry points are named `README.md` and use accessible wrappers for all informative emojis.
